## About

**CRISPR** is the technical and innovation club of **IIIT Nagpur**.

We focus on building practical projects that solve real problems and provide hands-on experience with modern technologies.

Rather than limiting ourselves to theoretical learning, we encourage students to **experiment, build, deploy, and learn through real-world development**.

> **The goal is simple: build things that work and learn by creating.**

---

## What We Do

CRISPR works across different areas of technology and innovation:

* 🚀 Build real-world software and technical projects
* 💡 Explore innovative ideas and experimental builds
* 🛠️ Develop and maintain useful internal tools and platforms
* 🤝 Collaborate on hackathons and open-source projects
* ⚙️ Support technical initiatives across the institute
* 🌐 Build platforms and systems used by students and the institute
* 🧪 Experiment with new technologies and development approaches

Most of our work happens through **active project repositories, collaboration, and hands-on development**.

## How to Contribute

We encourage students to contribute through **projects, ideas, code, and experimentation**.

### You can contribute by:

1. Exploring an existing repository
2. Identifying an issue or improvement
3. Forking the repository and building your solution
4. Submitting a pull request
5. Proposing a new project or technical idea

We value contributions that are **useful, practical, well-built, and innovative**.

---

## Joining CRISPR

If you enjoy building real projects, experimenting with technology, and working with other developers, CRISPR is the place to get involved.

You can:

* Explore our repositories
* Contribute to existing projects
* Start something new
* Collaborate with other students
* Turn ideas into working systems

**Build. Experiment. Deploy. Learn.**
